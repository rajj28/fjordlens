Beskrivelse

Dette er en utstillingsmodell, selges som den er og hentes på showroomet i Oslo. Det er kundens ansvar å se over produktet, det er ikke retur på utstillingsmodeller.

Model 711 was designed by Fredrik A. Kayser for LK Hjelle; it is a chair that has been a staple of Norwegian furniture design since its creation and is still handcrafted at the factory in Sykkylven. This display model features a black oak frame and a seat upholstered in black Prescott leather—a combination that only grows more beautiful with age.

This is the chair that has been on display in our showroom, with the slight signs of wear that come with use. Only one available.
