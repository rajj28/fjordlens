Our Vision

 

Avito aims to build a bridge between the business and the information technology world, realizing the value of data, and begin treating it as any other strategic asset they possess. We ensure that organisations make their business decisions truly fuelled by data and we accelerate the use of data driven decision making.

Our History

 

Avito was established in 2006.

The company provides consultancy services to various industries, and focuses its core business on providing strategic advisory and implementation solutions to its clients.

Avito's philosophy is driven by value-adding, where every action and decision is focused on improving its customer's productivity and efficiency through data.

Avito maintains its competitiveness based on core business domains, by actively understanding the business sectors it is involved in. Through understanding and experience, the company is able to help address and solve fundamental challenges across these various industries.

In 2024, VIEW Group acquired Avito to strengthen its position within BI, Analytics and ESG. VIEW Group is a rapidly growing Nordic player in accounting technology (AccounTech), finance and payroll services and IT outsourcing.
