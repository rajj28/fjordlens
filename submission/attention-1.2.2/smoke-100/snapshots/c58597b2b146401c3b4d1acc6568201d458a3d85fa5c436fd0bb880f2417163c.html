Privacy declaration

Avito is dedicated to the preservation of your privacy and the protection of your personal information.

Our data protection policy delineates the comprehensive measures we adopt in the collection, handling, and safeguarding of your personal data, in alignment with industry best practices and regulatory standards.

What is Personal data

Personal data refers to any information relating to an identified or identifiable individual. For a detailed breakdown of the types of personal data Avito processes, alongside other crucial details about our handling of personal data, please read the information provided below.

The protection of privacy aims to prevent the improper use of personal information. The management of personal data falls under the jurisdiction of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), a legislative framework designed to safeguard privacy, personal integrity, and ensure the high quality of personal data. For further insights into privacy matters, please visit Datatilsynet.

Data Collection Practices
Our utilization of HubSpot enables us to quantify user engagement and website traffic, thereby facilitating our ongoing development and enhancement efforts through insights into website usage. HubSpot employs cookies to gather information about traffic in an anonymized manner. This information helps us see how people use our site in different ways, like:

  • Total page views
  • Number of visits
  • Entry methods utilized by visitors
  • Browser specifications
  • Duration of time spent on the site
Your Entitlements

You are entitled to:

  • Request information;
  • Obtain access to your data;
  • Request correction of your data;
  • Request deletion of your data;
  • Ask for limitation on processing your data;
  • Exercise your right to data portability;
  • Lodge a complaint with a regulatory body; and
  • Object to certain uses of your data.
Cookies

Cookies are small data files saved on your computer, phone, or other devices. They help websites remember your actions or preferences over time.

We utilize cookies and similar identifiers, combined with personal data, to enhance your experience of our services. This includes verifying your identity and facilitating access to valuable information. In our marketing efforts and other communications, we may create profiles to tailor our messages to what we believe interests you most.

Additional Applicable Laws

Avito operates under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), which mandates our legal responsibilities to:

  • Retain documentation/information (including personal data) in adherence to the Archives Act for durations extending beyond the initial purposes of data processing; and
  • Grant access to documentation/information (including personal data).

The legal framework for the disclosure of personal data is further elaborated in the section concerning Avito’s data processing practices previously mentioned.
