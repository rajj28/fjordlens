Launch  & Recovery Systems

DWT can help you with mobilization and spooling jobs with our highly qualified team.


Our electrical spooling unit can accomodate an umbilical up to 55mm and 20T tension. The operation is controlled digitally and allows a very precise operation.


The unit is easily transported on a container frame and can be stowed perfectly in a Boeing 737.
